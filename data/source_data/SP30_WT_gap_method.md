# São Paulo 30-minute WT registry/equipment gap summary

The definite gap is an origin that is lower-bound covered by WT within 30 minutes in the Registry (R) arm and not upper-bound covered in the Equipment-SUS (E) arm. Therefore, origins with unresolved E-arm technical errors are excluded from the definite gap; their weighted population and a possible-gap total are reported separately. This conservative comparison preserves the distinction between route failures and noncoverage.

Group denominators include the full main-sample frame and use frozen `main_sp.csv` design weights. SES groups are the existing tract-income strata Q1–Q5 plus Unknown. Weighted medians use only definite-gap origins. Distance is straight-line great-circle (haversine) distance to the nearest R-qualified Equipment-SUS site (R=1 and E=1; 89 sites), not routed travel distance.

The accompanying reference CSV copies the existing weighted WT 30-minute R/E coverage estimates and paired D from the completed analysis tables. No route calculation or new confidence interval is added here.
